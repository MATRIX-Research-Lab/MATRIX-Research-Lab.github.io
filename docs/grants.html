Grants

Research support, current and past.

Active

Current funding

  • Quantifying and Predicting the Health Impacts of Wildfires and Extreme Heat Events Among End-Stage Kidney Disease Patients

    Role: Contact Principal Investigator (Multiple-PI, with Amir Sapkota) · NIEHS / NIH · 1R21ES037480-01

    Sep 2025 – Sep 2027

    Links wildfire smoke and extreme heat exposure to hospitalization and mortality risk in a highly vulnerable dialysis population, and builds predictive models to support early warning.

    Active R21 NIEHS/NIH

  • Sustaining and Diversifying an Environmental Health Research Program on Late-Onset Asthma: Pilot Microbiome and Metabolome Profiling

    Role: Principal Investigator · MPower Early Scholars Investment Fund, University of Maryland

    Jul 2026 – Jun 2027

    Pilot microbiome and metabolome profiling to extend the lab's environmental health work into late-onset asthma.

    Active UMD

  • AWareness Against Health ThReats of Climate ChangE (AWARE)

    Role: Co-Investigator (PI: Amir Sapkota) · NSF / Belmont Forum · RISE2538018

    Aug 2025 – Aug 2028

    An international Belmont Forum collaboration on climate-sensitive health threats. The lab contributes statistical and machine-learning methodology for exposure–outcome modeling.

    Active NSF / Belmont Forum

  • The Air We Share: A Public Health Revolution for the 21st Century

    Role: Co-Investigator (PI: Donald K. Milton) · Grand Challenges 2.0 Team Grant, University of Maryland

    Jul 2026 – Jun 2029

    A university-wide team award on indoor air quality and airborne transmission, with the lab leading analysis of high-dimensional bioaerosol and microbiome data.

    Active UMD

  • Centers of Excellence for Influenza Research and Response

    Role: Co-Investigator (PI: Donald K. Milton) · Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai (prime: NIAID / NIH) · 75N93021C00014

    Apr 2021 – Mar 2028

    Federal contract supporting influenza research and response, including causal analysis of respiratory virus transmission in residential cohorts.

    Active NIAID/NIH Resulting publications
